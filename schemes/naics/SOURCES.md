# NAICS scheme: sources

Generated by `src/shelfmark/schemes/naics/builder` (`naics-scheme build`); do not edit by hand.
`naics.json` is scheme `naics@2022` with 2125 classes and validates against
`src/shelfmark/schemas/scheme.schema.json` and `shelfmark_core::scheme::SchemeDoc`.

## Rebuild

```sh
cd src/shelfmark/schemes/naics/builder
cargo run --release -- fetch --cache /tmp/naics-sources   # network; rewrites sources.lock.json
cargo run --release -- build --cache /tmp/naics-sources   # offline; deterministic from the lock
cargo run --release -- check --cache /tmp/naics-sources   # fails if naics.json or SOURCES.md is stale
```

`build` refuses any cached file whose sha256 differs from `sources.lock.json`.

## License

The inputs are the United States edition of NAICS 2022, issued by the Office of Management
and Budget and published by the US Census Bureau. As works of the US government they are
not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105), so `naics.json` may ship
inside an open-source binary or package; no downloader is needed. Credit the source as
"Source: U.S. Census Bureau, 2022 NAICS". The Canadian and Mexican editions (Statistics
Canada, INEGI) carry their own terms and are not used. The derived `naics.json` keeps the
titles and index entries verbatim apart from collapsing runs of whitespace.
